Making / Article 1

There is already enough. Start there.

By SadAge · Editorial draft
19 September 2026

A garment arrives with a past: a worn edge, a fading colour, a shape that has stopped being fashionable. These are starting points for a different kind of work.

Read the full note +
Working with an existing piece means making decisions in response to what is already there. A seam changes the position of a print. A mark becomes part of a composition. A repair stays visible. The result can carry both its earlier life and the decisions made in the workshop.

Images / Article 2

A sign on an ordinary body

By SadAge · Editorial draft
19 September 2026

A small image changes how a plain shirt is read. The body stays familiar; the message moves.

Read the full note +
Placement is part of the image. A graphic can sit quietly at the chest, spill into a seam or occupy an entire back. Scale, empty space and the colour of the garment all take part in the conversation.

Observations / Article 3

Keep the evidence

By SadAge · Editorial draft
19 September 2026

Fading, loose edges and uneven surfaces keep a record of use. Looking closely is a way of finding a new beginning.

Read the full note +
An archive does not have to be a room full of untouched things. It can be a collection of garments still being worn, repaired and changed. The next version does not erase the last one.
