Cookie policy

Updated 21 September 2026

Your choice

Optional analytics are off until you accept. Rejecting them does not prevent browsing or buying. Use to change your choice. Withdrawal stops future analytics loading and reloads the page if tracking was active; it does not undo information already sent.

Browser storage

sadage-analytics-consent-v1 is a local-storage preference recording your choice and its date. The site asks again after 180 days. sadage-objects-language remembers your selected Objects language until you clear browser storage. These preferences are not advertising identifiers.

Analytics cookies

On pages using Google Analytics, accepting analytics permits Google to use identifiers such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish visits and sessions. Their default expiry can be up to two years. On pages using it, Microsoft Clarity may use _clck (normally one year) and _clsk (normally one day) to connect interactions and sessions. Provider settings and browser limits can change actual lifetimes.

Rejecting analytics removes accessible first-party analytics cookies at the site root. You can also clear site data through your browser to remove stored preferences or cookies that this site cannot access.

Other services

Stripe, BTCPay, WhatsApp and Instagram may use their own necessary or optional cookies when you open their pages. Choices on sadage.com do not control storage on other sites. Google Fonts requests do not depend on analytics consent; see our Privacy policy for network data and service providers.
