Open Call

For people with an image, a skill, a question or a way of looking.

Make something
with us.

A conversation can be the beginning of a piece.

Photography, film, writing, print, garment work and unexpected combinations. Tell us what you have in mind.

Draft invitation / collaboration terms are agreed individually before work begins.

Send a proposal.

01

Introduce yourself

Your name or preferred credit, what you do and how to reach you.

02

Show the idea

A short description and a link to a few relevant examples. Include what you would like to make with SadAge.

03

Set the scale

Tell us your availability, location if relevant, and the resources or budget the idea needs.

Before we begin.

Share work you own or have permission to share. Submitting an idea does not grant permission to publish or reproduce it.

Scope, credit, usage rights, costs, payment and timing should be agreed together in writing before any work starts. A proposal is an introduction, not a confirmed commission.
